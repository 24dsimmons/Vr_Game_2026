# Avengers Tool Simulator 

**Genre:** Simulation 

**Platform:** Meta Quest

**Player Count:** Single

**Target Audience** 12+

**Description:** A Vr immersion game where you get to try on different Weapons of the Avengers utilizing Meta's hand tracking and pass-through

**Players Will:**
- interact with different models
- Switch from An actual model with color to a wire-frame styled view.

**Game Design:**
Players be immersed with different Weapons of the Avengers and change the properties of each one.
